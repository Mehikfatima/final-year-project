Tour Overview

Fairy Meadows is one of Pakistan’s most beautiful adventure destinations, famous for its magical view of Nanga Parbat. This tour is best for adventure lovers, campers, photographers and hiking groups. Package includes jeep ride, camping, bonfire and hiking options.

Suggested Day Plan

Day 1

Departure to Chilas / Raikot

Pickup from selected city, travel through scenic route and night stay near Chilas or Raikot.

Day 2

Jeep Ride + Hiking to Fairy Meadows

Day 3

Nanga Parbat View + Bonfire

Free day for photography, viewpoint hike, camping, group activities and night bonfire.

Day 4

Optional Beyal Camp Hike

Optional hiking package towards Beyal Camp or rest day at Fairy Meadows.

Day 5

Return Journey

Hike back to Tattu Village, jeep return to Raikot Bridge and travel back to starting city.
