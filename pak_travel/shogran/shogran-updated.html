Tour Overview

Shogran is one of Pakistan’s most beautiful weekend destinations, famous for its green forests, cool weather, mountain views, and Siri Paye Meadows. This public group tour is best for families, students, friends, photographers, and nature lovers. Package includes group transport, hotel stay, sightseeing, bonfire, and optional jeep ride to Siri Paye.
