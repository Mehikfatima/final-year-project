Tour Overview

Multan Heritage Tour is designed for families, students and heritage lovers. The package covers famous Sufi shrines, old city bazaars, Multani blue pottery workshops, Ghanta Ghar area and food streets. Payment changes according to starting city distance, selected hotel and selected vehicle because fuel/petrol and comfort level are different.

Suggested Day Plan

Day 1

Departure & Multan City Arrival

Pickup from selected city and travel towards Multan. After hotel check-in, evening visit to famous Food Streets, Hussain Agahi Bazaar and local markets.

Day 2

Shrines & Blue Pottery Tour

Visit Shah Rukn-e-Alam Shrine, Bahauddin Zakariya Shrine, Multan Fort area and famous Blue Pottery market. Photography, shopping and cultural exploration included.

Day 3

Breakfast & Return Journey

Breakfast at hotel and checkout. Return travel towards your selected city with refreshment stops on the route.
