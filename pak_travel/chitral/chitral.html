Tour Overview

Chitral is one of Pakistan’s most unique cultural and mountain destinations, famous for Kalash Valley, Garam Chashma, Chitral Fort and Shandur Pass. This tour is best for families, culture lovers, photographers and adventure travelers.

Suggested Day Plan

Day 1

Departure to Chitral

Pickup from selected city and travel through scenic mountain routes towards Chitral for overnight hotel stay.

Day 2

Chitral City + Chitral Fort Visit

Explore Chitral city, Chitral Fort, local bazaar and cultural heritage sites with photography stops.

Day 3

Kalash Valley Cultural Tour

Visit Kalash Valley, experience unique Kalash culture, traditional houses, colorful dress and local lifestyle.

Day 4

Garam Chashma Visit

Visit Garam Chashma hot spring area, enjoy mountain views, nature photography and peaceful valley scenery.

Day 5

Shandur Pass Tour

Visit Shandur Pass, famous for its high-altitude polo ground, wide landscapes and adventure views.

Day 6

Return Journey

Breakfast at hotel and return travel towards your selected city with short refreshment stops on the route.
