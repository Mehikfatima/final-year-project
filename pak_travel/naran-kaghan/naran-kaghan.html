Tour Overview

Naran Kaghan is one of Pakistan’s most attractive mountain destinations. This package is perfect for families, couples and groups who want lakes, rivers, cool weather, scenic drives and comfortable hotel stays.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Departure to Naran

Start from your selected city. Travel through Balakot and Kaghan Valley with Kunhar River views.

Day 2

Saif-ul-Malook Lake

Visit the famous lake by jeep, enjoy photography, boating options and local food points.

Day 3

Shogran & Siri Paye

Explore green meadows, mountain viewpoints and optional jeep adventure.

Day 4

Lalazar / Babusar Top

Visit Lalazar meadows or Babusar Top depending on road and weather conditions.

Day 5

Return Journey

Drive back with photo stops and memories of Naran Kaghan.

Famous Places in Naran Kaghan

Image/card par click karen, map usi place ki location par update ho jayega.

Saif-ul-Malook Lake

Famous alpine lake near Naran with jeep access, boating options and mountain views.

Babusar Top

High mountain pass and viewpoint, usually open in summer depending on weather and road condition.

Lalazar Meadows

Green meadows, mountain scenery and photography point near Naran Valley.

Siri Paye Meadows

Beautiful meadow trip from Shogran with jeep adventure and panoramic valley views.

Kunhar River

Popular riverside sightseeing, trout fish points and scenic stops along the route.

Jalkhad

Quiet mountain area on the way to Babusar with cool weather and scenic drive experience.
