Tour Overview

Swat Valley is known as the Switzerland of Pakistan. This package is ideal for families, couples and groups who want green valleys, snowy peaks, waterfalls, rivers, chairlift rides, Kalam scenery and comfortable hotel stays.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Departure to Swat

Start from your selected city. Travel through motorway route and reach Mingora/Fizagat with scenic river views.

Day 2

Malam Jabba Visit

Enjoy chairlift, zipline options, snow views in winter and photography at Malam Jabba.

Day 3

Bahrain & Kalam

Drive towards Bahrain and Kalam, explore riverside views, bazaars and mountain scenery.

Day 4

Ushu Forest / Mahodand Lake

Visit Ushu Forest and Mahodand Lake depending on road and weather conditions. Jeep charges may be separate.

Day 5

Return Journey

Breakfast, checkout and return with short photo stops on the way.
